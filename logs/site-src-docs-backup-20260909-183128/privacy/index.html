Privacy

What we do with what you send us

The whole notice, in plain English, on one page, covering both forms on this site. If anything is unclear, write to info@matchworkroofing.com and we will answer in the same plain English.

What we collect

Only what a form asks for, and what you choose to send

The estimate form

Used on most pages. It asks for your name, email address, phone number, street address, city, and anything you write in the notes box. That is the whole list.

It also carries two values you never fill in: the trade the page you submitted from belongs to, and the referral details below.

If you email us instead, we have what is in the email and whatever you attach. Photographs are treated exactly like an uploaded file.

We do not ask for and will not accept financial account details, card numbers or a government identification number through this site or by email.

The realtor form

Used on the realtor page, because a transaction needs details a homeowner estimate does not:

  • Agent name, and brokerage and office
  • Mobile number and email address
  • Property address
  • Your role: listing agent, buyer agent or property manager
  • The inspection contingency or remedy deadline, as a date
  • The closing or list date, as a date
  • Trades needed: roofing, siding, gutters, masonry, a roof inspection report, or not sure yet
  • An uploaded inspection report or photographs, as PDF, JPG or PNG
  • Lender type: FHA, VA, USDA, conventional, cash or unknown
  • MLS ID, optional
  • Preferred visit window, optional
  • Who pays: seller, buyer or unknown, optional
  • Anything else about the deal, optional

That form never asks for the insurance carrier, or for anything about a buyer's or seller's personal finances. Lender type is there because a loan programme changes what an appraiser or underwriter expects to see remedied. We use it to scope the work and do not pass it on.

Both forms carry one hidden decoy field, left empty for spam bots to fill in. A submission that fills it is discarded, and no human ever types in it.

Referral details

The UTM parameters and the landing page

If you arrive from a link carrying marketing parameters — the ones that start with utm: source, medium, campaign, term and content — this site reads them from the address bar and stores them in your browser's session storage for that tab. It also stores the path of the page you submitted a form from.

Those values are written into hidden fields and travel with your submission, so we know which page or advert prompted you to write. That is all they are used for.

Session storage lives in your browser tab, not on our server, and not in a cookie that follows you elsewhere. It is gone when the tab closes. Arrive without those parameters and nothing is stored.

We do not use them to build an audience or to retarget you on another site.

Uploaded files

What happens to an inspection report you upload

The rule, plainly

An uploaded inspection report or photograph set is used only to scope the work you asked us to scope. It is read by the person preparing your estimate and, if the job goes ahead, by the crew lead. It is kept as long as the transaction needs it — through your remedy deadline and closing — and after that only as part of the job file, if we did the work. If we did no work it is deleted when the enquiry closes, and sooner than that whenever you ask. It is never used for marketing, never published, and never passed to a brokerage, a lender, an insurance carrier or another contractor.

An inspection report holds more than roof remarks: the property address, often the seller's and buyer's names, and usually photographs of the inside of a home. We treat the whole document as confidential, not only the pages that concern us.

To have a file deleted, write to info@matchworkroofing.com from the address you submitted with, or give us the property address and the date you sent it. We confirm in writing once it is gone. Deletion removes the file itself, not only the record that you sent one, so keep your own copy.

Why we have it

To answer you, and to price the work

  • To reply to you, which we aim to do within one business day.
  • To find the property, measure the roof and elevations, and prepare your estimate.
  • To read an inspection report and separate a legitimate repair from what should be monitored and what is not our trade.
  • To schedule around a showing, a contingency deadline or a closing date.
  • To carry out the work if you go ahead, and keep the job file afterwards.

That is all of it. We do not build a marketing list out of your details, add you to a newsletter you did not ask for, or send follow-up campaigns after a conversation ends.

If a claim is involved, what you send to claims@matchworkroofing.com is used for that claim file and nothing else.

Sharing

We do not sell your information

We have never sold customer information and we are not going to. It is not traded, rented or passed to lead-buying services, marketing companies or data brokers. We do not buy leads either.

Two narrow situations put any of it in front of somebody outside Matchwork, and both happen only because you asked for the thing that requires it:

  • A supplier or crew member who needs the address, to deliver material to the right house or turn up at it.
  • Your insurance carrier, if you have asked us to send documentation into an open claim of yours. We do not open a claim on your behalf.

Nobody else. Not an advertising network, not a software vendor who can read your messages, not another contractor.

The website itself

Cookies, tracking and what a page loads

This site sets no advertising cookie and runs no cross-site tracking pixel. There is no consent banner because there is nothing to consent to. The only thing stored in your browser is the referral detail above, cleared when the tab closes.

Pages load their typefaces from Google Fonts, so your browser makes a request to Google's servers when a page opens. Our host keeps ordinary server logs — which page was requested, when, roughly where from — and we do not tie them to individual people.

Visitor measurement here is a Google Tag Manager container, and today it is a placeholder. No real container identifier is set, so the loader does not run, no tag fires, and nothing goes to Google Analytics or anybody else from it.

When a real container is set, this section names it before it goes live rather than after, and says what it measures. Until then the honest answer to "do you track me here" is no.

Honest status

Neither form is connected to anything yet

Both forms currently run in demonstration mode. No submission endpoint is configured, so pressing the button validates the form in your own browser, shows you a confirmation on the page, and transmits nothing anywhere. Nothing reaches us that way today.

That is stated here rather than hidden, because a notice describing a data flow that does not exist would be worse than useless. Until the forms are connected, email is how to reach us: info@matchworkroofing.com for an estimate, claims@matchworkroofing.com for storm and insurance documentation. The contact page says what to include.

When an endpoint is switched on, this section changes first, and says where submissions go and who hosts them.

Your copy, and deleting it

How to ask us to delete what we hold

Write to info@matchworkroofing.com from the address you contacted us with, or give us the property address, and say what you want: a copy of what we hold, a correction, deletion of an uploaded report, or deletion of your enquiry and details. We confirm in writing when it is done. There is no form to fill in and no account to log into.

How long we keep things

An enquiry that does not become a job is deleted once it is clearly finished, or sooner if you ask. If we did work on your house we keep the job file and its photographs, because that is the record of what was installed and you may need it years from now. You can ask us to delete that too, and we will, but we will tell you first what you are giving up.

Once a file is deleted it is gone, photographs included. We cannot get it back for you afterwards.

Questions

Ask a person

Privacy questions go to the same inbox as everything else, and a person reads them. If this notice changes, the new version replaces this page and the build date at the foot of every page moves with it.
