The Luxury Wood-Shake Look

CertainTeed Presidential Shake for Central Ohio Homes

Presidential Shake is CertainTeed's luxury take on the classic cedar-shake roof: a multi-layer asphalt shingle sculpted and color-blended to read as hand-split wood from the street, without the upkeep, fire exposure, or short service life of real wood. It is the shingle we point to when the architecture calls for more than a standard architectural profile.

What It Looks Like

Deep Shadow Lines and Cedar Tones

Presidential Shake's tabs are cut and layered to throw the irregular shadow lines of a hand-split shake roof, and the color blends run through weathered browns, grays, and charcoals rather than flat single tones. On a steep roof — where the shingle is most of what you see from the street — the effect is close enough to cedar that most visitors will not clock it as asphalt.

The profile suits the traditional architecture across Upper Arlington, Bexley, and the older streets of Dublin, and it is a natural fit for the larger custom homes going up around New Albany.

Who It Suits

Homes Where the Roof Is the Statement

Presidential Shake makes sense when the roof plane is large and visible — steep gables, turrets, dormers — and the owner wants a roof that elevates the whole elevation rather than disappearing into it. It is also the practical answer for anyone who loves the cedar-shake aesthetic but does not want wood's fire exposure, maintenance cycle, or insurance complications in Central Ohio's storm seasons.

If hail is your primary concern, the family's Class 4 variants — Presidential Shake TL and Presidential Shake IR — keep the look and add the highest published impact rating.

Frequently Asked Questions

Presidential Shake vs. Presidential Shake TL and IR — what's the difference?

All three share the hand-split wood-shake look. Standard Presidential Shake carries a UL 2218 Class 3 impact rating. Presidential Shake TL uses a triple-laminate construction and Presidential Shake IR is the impact-resistant variant — both carry a UL 2218 Class 4 impact rating, the highest published. If your neighborhood sees regular hail, we will price the Class 4 versions alongside the standard so you can compare.

Why choose Presidential Shake over real cedar shake?

You keep the look and lose the liabilities: Presidential Shake carries a UL Class A fire rating — the highest for roofing — and never splits, curls, rots, or grows moss the way wood does in Ohio's freeze-thaw and humidity. It also opens up manufacturer warranty coverage from CertainTeed that a natural wood roof simply does not have.

Who handles the manufacturer coverage on my shingles?

Manufacturer warranties are provided and administered by CertainTeed — ask us which manufacturer's warranty applies to the shingles on your estimate, and review the full terms on CertainTeed's warranty page.
