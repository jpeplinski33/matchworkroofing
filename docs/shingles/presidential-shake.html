The Luxury Wood-Shake Look

Presidential Shake

by CertainTeed

Presidential Shake is CertainTeed's luxury take on the classic cedar-shake roof: a multi-layer asphalt shingle sculpted and color-blended to read as hand-split wood from the street, without the upkeep, fire exposure, or short service life of real wood. It is the shingle we point to when the architecture calls for more than a standard architectural profile. MATCHWORK™ installs Presidential Shake across Central Ohio. What It Looks Like

Deep Shadow Lines and Cedar Tones

Presidential Shake's tabs are cut and layered to throw the irregular shadow lines of a hand-split shake roof, and the color blends run through weathered browns, grays, and charcoals rather than flat single tones. On a steep roof, where the shingle is most of what you see from the street, it reads as cedar shake.

The profile suits the traditional architecture across Upper Arlington, Bexley, and the older streets of Dublin, and it is a natural fit for the larger custom homes going up around New Albany.

Who It Suits

Large, Visible Roof Planes

Presidential Shake makes sense when the roof plane is large and visible (steep gables, turrets, dormers) and the owner wants the roof to be part of the design, not background. It is also the practical answer for anyone who loves the cedar-shake aesthetic but does not want wood's fire exposure, maintenance cycle, or insurance complications in Central Ohio's storm seasons.

Frequently Asked Questions

Presidential Shake vs. Presidential Shake TL and IR: what's the difference?

All three share the hand-split wood-shake look. Standard Presidential Shake is a two-piece laminate with a UL 2218 Class 3 impact rating. Presidential Shake TL is a three-piece laminate, and Presidential Shake IR is CertainTeed's impact-resistant version; both carry a UL 2218 Class 4 impact rating. Ask us and we will price any of the three on your estimate.

Why choose Presidential Shake over real cedar shake?

You keep the look and lose the liabilities: Presidential Shake carries a UL Class A fire rating, the highest for roofing. It also opens up manufacturer warranty coverage from CertainTeed that a natural wood roof simply does not have.

Who handles the manufacturer coverage on my shingles?

Manufacturer warranties are provided and administered by CertainTeed. Ask us which CertainTeed warranty applies to your roof, and see the full terms on CertainTeed's warranty page.
