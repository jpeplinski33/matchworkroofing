The Look

What Landmark Premium Looks Like — and Who It Suits

From the street, Landmark Premium reads as the familiar dimensional Landmark profile — staggered tabs and strong shadow lines — but the extra weight gives the roof a thicker, more deliberate edge at the rakes and eaves. It is a natural match for the brick colonials and stone-front homes common in New Albany and Dublin, where a heavier-looking roof carries the architecture better than a lightweight shingle.

It suits homeowners who want the highest impact classification available in a standard-asphalt shingle without moving to a rubberized or designer line. Central Ohio's spring hail seasons are the practical argument: UL 2218 Class 4 is the top rung of the industry's impact test, and some insurers offer premium discounts for Class 4 roofs — ask your agent what your policy offers.

If you like the Class 4 rating but want a shingle that also flexes in cold weather and grips its granules harder under hail, compare it against Landmark ClimateFlex at your estimate — we will lay both out side by side.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does a Class 4 impact rating actually mean?

It is the top score under UL 2218, a standardized test that drops steel balls of increasing size onto the shingle to simulate hail. Class 4 is the highest of the four classes — the shingle withstood the largest test ball without cracking. For hail-prone Central Ohio neighborhoods, it is the strongest published impact rating a shingle can carry, and some insurers offer premium discounts for Class 4 roofs. Ask your insurance agent what your policy offers.

Should I choose Landmark Premium or Landmark ClimateFlex?

Both carry the UL 2218 Class 4 impact rating. Landmark Premium gets there as a heavier, top-weight standard-asphalt laminate with the classic Landmark look. Landmark ClimateFlex gets there with a rubberized polymer-modified asphalt that also adds cold-weather pliability and enhanced granule adhesion. If you want maximum weight and the traditional profile, that is Premium; if you want the added flexibility of a rubberized shingle, that is ClimateFlex. We can price both on the same estimate.

Who handles the manufacturer coverage on my shingles?

Manufacturer warranties are provided and administered by CertainTeed — ask us which manufacturer's warranty applies to the shingles on your estimate, and review the full terms on CertainTeed's warranty page.
