What It Looks Like

A Designer Look That Shrugs Off Storms

NorthGate ClimateFlex carries the depth and shadow lines of a designer shingle — the kind of roof that reads as an upgrade from the street, not a compromise you made for impact resistance. The Max Def blends lean into the deep charcoals, weathered browns, and slate grays that pair well with the brick and stone elevations common across Dublin, New Albany, and Upper Arlington.

Because color availability varies by region, we confirm the current Ohio palette and bring physical samples to your estimate rather than asking you to pick from a screen.

Who It Suits

Homes in the Hail Path

Central Ohio sits in a genuine hail corridor — most Columbus-area neighborhoods see damaging hail within any given decade, and some subdivisions get hit far more often. If your roof has been replaced once already after a storm, or your insurer is steering the conversation toward impact-resistant materials, NorthGate ClimateFlex is the CertainTeed shingle built for exactly that scenario.

It also makes sense on larger, steeper roofs where a second storm claim would mean another full replacement — the rubberized mat is engineered to take the impact that cracks a standard shingle.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does a Class 4 impact rating actually mean?

Class 4 is the highest level in UL 2218, the standard impact test for roofing. CertainTeed's impact-resistant shingles — including NorthGate ClimateFlex — are tested to withstand a two-inch steel ball dropped from twenty feet without cracking. No shingle is hail-proof, but Class 4 is the toughest published rating, and some insurers offer premium discounts for Class 4 roofs; ask your insurance agent what your policy offers.

NorthGate ClimateFlex or Landmark ClimateFlex — what's the difference?

Both are rubberized, polymer-modified shingles with a UL 2218 Class 4 impact rating. NorthGate ClimateFlex is the premium tier: it comes in CertainTeed's Max Def color palette and carries a 30-year StreakFighter algae-resistance warranty, where Landmark ClimateFlex carries a 25-year StreakFighter warranty in the classic Landmark look. We price both on your estimate so you can compare.

Who handles the manufacturer coverage on my shingles?

Manufacturer warranties are provided and administered by CertainTeed — ask us which manufacturer's warranty applies to the shingles on your estimate, and review the full terms on CertainTeed's warranty page.
