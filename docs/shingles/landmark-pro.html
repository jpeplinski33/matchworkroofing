Manufacturer Specifications

Performance, Protection, and Coverage

The numbers below are CertainTeed's published specifications for Landmark PRO.

Performance

Built for Ohio Weather

  • UL Class A fire rating, the highest rating for roofing
  • UL 2218 Class 3 impact resistance
  • 110 mph wind resistance; CertainTeed offers upgrades up to 160 mph when the roof is installed with specified CertainTeed starter and hip & ridge accessories over a full tear-off
  • NailTrak high-visibility nailing area for accurate fastening
Protection

Color That Holds Up

  • Max Def colors with richer, deeper blends and stronger shadow lines
  • 30-year StreakFighter algae-resistance warranty from CertainTeed against black streak staining when installed with CertainTeed algae-resistant hip and ridge
  • Dual-layer laminated construction, heavier than standard Landmark
  • Screen displays approximate colors. We bring physical samples to your estimate
Coverage

Backed by CertainTeed

  • CertainTeed backs Landmark PRO with a lifetime limited transferable warranty
  • 10-year SureStart protection from CertainTeed
  • Manufacturer warranties are provided and administered by CertainTeed

The Lineup

The CertainTeed Lineup We Install

Landmark PRO is the shingle we install most, but we install the full CertainTeed lineup. The differences come down to impact rating, color depth, algae-resistance coverage, and architectural look.

Architectural Shingles

Landmark

The standard-bearer architectural shingle. Class 3 impact rating, 25-year StreakFighter algae-resistance warranty, and a wide CertainTeed color range.

Landmark PRO (installed most)

The shingle we install most. Heavier laminate, Class 3 impact rating, Max Def colors, and a 30-year StreakFighter warranty, five years longer than standard Landmark.

NorthGate ClimateFlex

CertainTeed's premium rubberized shingle: SBS polymer-modified asphalt with a UL 2218 Class 4 impact rating and Max Def colors.

Landmark Premium

The heaviest of CertainTeed's classic-look Landmark shingles, with a UL 2218 Class 4 impact rating and a 30-year StreakFighter warranty.

Landmark ClimateFlex

A rubberized Landmark: polymer-modified asphalt with a UL 2218 Class 4 impact rating, enhanced granule adhesion, and cold-weather pliability.

Landmark Solaris

Cool-roof colors engineered to reflect solar heat, in the standard Landmark profile with a Class 3 impact rating.

Designer & Luxury Shingles

Presidential Shake

Luxury multi-layer shingle with the look of hand-split wood shake. Triple-laminate (TL) and impact-resistant (IR) versions carry a UL 2218 Class 4 impact rating.

Grand Manor

Multi-layer luxury shingle with the look of quarried slate, suited to larger homes and traditional architecture.

Carriage House, Highland Slate & Belmont

CertainTeed's slate-look designer lines: Carriage House scalloped edges, Highland Slate random-tab profiles, and Belmont deep shadow lines for distinctive high-end roofs.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Landmark PRO worth the upgrade over standard Landmark?

For most Central Ohio homes, yes. You get a heavier shingle, the Max Def color palette, and five extra years of StreakFighter algae-resistance coverage for a modest difference in material cost. We price both on your estimate so you can compare.

Should I consider a Class 4 shingle in Ohio?

Landmark PRO carries a UL 2218 Class 3 impact rating, which suits most Central Ohio homes. If you want a Class 4 shingle, ask us and we will price one next to Landmark PRO.

Who handles the manufacturer coverage on my shingles?

Manufacturer warranties are provided and administered by CertainTeed. Ask us which CertainTeed warranty applies to your roof, and see the full terms on CertainTeed's warranty page.
