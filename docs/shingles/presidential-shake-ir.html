What It Looks Like

The Wood-Shake Look With a Class 4 Impact Rating

From the curb, Presidential Shake IR is indistinguishable from the standard Presidential look: staggered, sculpted tabs with rustic edges that read as hand-split cedar rather than a flat asphalt pattern. The difference is the impact rating.

CertainTeed certifies Presidential Shake IR to UL 2218 Class 4, the highest class in the test.

Who It Suits

Homes With Large, Visible Roofs

Presidential Shake IR makes the most sense on homes where the roof is a major design feature, the traditional two-stories of Upper Arlington, custom builds in Dublin and New Albany, and where the owner wants the toughest version of the look they love.

Manufacturer Coverage

The CertainTeed Warranty on Presidential Shake IR

CertainTeed backs Presidential Shake IR with a lifetime limited transferable warranty against manufacturing defects on residential applications, and a 50-year limited transferable warranty on group-owned or commercial applications. CertainTeed also includes 10-year SureStart protection, a 30-year StreakFighter algae-resistance warranty (when installed with CertainTeed algae-resistant hip and ridge), and a 15-year 110 mph wind-resistance warranty, with an upgrade to 130 mph available when the roof is installed with CertainTeed starter and CertainTeed hip & ridge accessories.

Manufacturer warranties are provided and administered by CertainTeed. Read the full terms on CertainTeed's warranty page or in CertainTeed's 2026 asphalt shingle limited warranty (PDF), and ask us which CertainTeed warranty applies to your roof.

Frequently Asked Questions

What makes Presidential Shake IR different from standard Presidential Shake?

The impact build. Standard Presidential Shake carries a UL 2218 Class 3 impact rating, while Presidential Shake IR carries a Class 4 rating, the highest class. The sculpted wood-shake look and the CertainTeed warranty stack are otherwise shared across the line.

Who handles the manufacturer coverage on my shingles?

Manufacturer warranties are provided and administered by CertainTeed. Ask us which CertainTeed warranty applies to your roof, and see the full terms on CertainTeed's warranty page.
