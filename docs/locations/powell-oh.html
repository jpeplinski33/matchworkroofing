Roofs in Powell

A Town That Grew Up After 1990

About four in five Powell homes were built in 1990 or later, and more than half in 2000 or later. A roof put on in the 2000s is now around twenty years old, the age when repair or replacement becomes a real question. The answer depends on what is under the shingles as much as on the shingles themselves: the deck, the flashing at walls and chimneys, and the attic ventilation.

Housing ages: U.S. Census Bureau, American Community Survey 2020-2024 5-year estimates (data.census.gov).

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you help with insurance claims?

Yes. We inspect and photograph every item of damage, write a line-by-line estimate in the same software insurance carriers use, meet your adjuster on the roof, and send supplements with photos and code references when the carrier's estimate leaves something out. When new shingles will not match the rest of your roof, we put Ohio's matching rule in front of the carrier.

Do you replace gutters and siding?

Yes. We replace and repair siding and gutters, install gutter guards, and can include any of it in your roofing estimate.
