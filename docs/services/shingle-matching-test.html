Shingle Matching & Product Identification

When a roof repair is proposed, the question is whether a matching shingle is still available. A sample of your shingle can be sent to an independent laboratory such as ITEL to identify the manufacturer and product and report whether it is still in production. The result is a document you and your insurer can refer to when discussing repair versus replacement.

What Gets Compared

Four Reasons a Repair Shingle May Not Match

A replacement shingle that is close is not always a match. These are the differences that matter when a repair is being considered:

Dimensional Mismatch

Shingles have been made in different sizes and exposures over the years. A new shingle of a different size does not lay in cleanly with the existing courses.

Granule Color Fade

Granules weather and shift in color with years of sun exposure. A factory-fresh shingle placed next to weathered ones usually stands out.

Product Discontinuation

Manufacturers retire product lines over time. When a product is discontinued, remaining stock and suitable repair options need to be checked.

Brittleness / Repairability

Older shingles can become brittle. Lifting them to slide in a replacement can crack the surrounding shingles, so a small repair can cause additional damage.
