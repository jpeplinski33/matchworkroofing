The Lineup

Good, Better, Best: Landmark vs. Landmark PRO vs. NorthGate ClimateFlex

All three are CertainTeed architectural shingles we install in Central Ohio. The differences come down to impact rating, color depth, and algae-resistance coverage.

Landmark®

The standard-bearer architectural shingle. Class 3 impact rating, 25-year StreakFighter® algae-resistance warranty, and a wide CertainTeed color range.

Landmark® PRO

Our value pick. Heavier laminate, Class 3 impact rating, 24 Max Def colors, and a 30-year StreakFighter® warranty — five years longer than standard Landmark.

NorthGate® ClimateFlex®

CertainTeed's premium rubberized shingle: SBS polymer-modified asphalt with a UL 2218 Class 4 impact rating and cold-weather pliability — the hail-belt upgrade for Ohio storm seasons.

Wondering whether Class 4 shingles make sense for your home? Read our guide to hail damage in Ohio, then ask us at your estimate.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Landmark PRO worth the upgrade over standard Landmark?

For most Central Ohio homes, yes. You get a heavier shingle, the Max Def color palette, and five extra years of StreakFighter® algae-resistance coverage for a modest difference in material cost. We price both on your estimate so you can compare.

Should I consider a Class 4 shingle in Ohio?

If your neighborhood sees regular hail, NorthGate® ClimateFlex® is worth a look — a rubberized shingle with a UL 2218 Class 4 impact rating, and some insurers offer premium discounts for Class 4 roofs. Ask your insurance agent what your policy offers.

Who handles the manufacturer coverage on my shingles?

Manufacturer warranties are provided and administered by CertainTeed — ask us which manufacturer's warranty applies to the shingles on your estimate, and review the full terms on CertainTeed's warranty page.
