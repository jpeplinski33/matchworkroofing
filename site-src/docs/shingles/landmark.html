The Look

What Landmark Looks Like — and Who It Suits

Landmark is the dimensional, wood-shake-inspired profile you see on the majority of architectural-shingle roofs around Columbus. Its two laminated layers create staggered shadow lines that read as depth from the street — a noticeable step up from flat three-tab shingles, and a natural fit for the colonials in Dublin, the capes and Tudors in Upper Arlington, and the newer two-stories filling in around New Albany and Westerville.

It suits homeowners who want a proven, widely stocked shingle with strong published ratings and no premium-line markup. Central Ohio's freeze-thaw winters and spring hail seasons are exactly the conditions its Class A fire rating, Class 3 impact rating, and 110 mph wind resistance are aimed at. If your neighborhood sees frequent hail and you want the highest impact classification, we will price a Class 4 option like Landmark Premium or Landmark ClimateFlex alongside it so you can compare real numbers.

Because Landmark is the baseline of the family, it is also the easiest line to match later — helpful if a future repair or addition needs shingles that sit comfortably next to the original roof.

Manufacturer Warranty

How CertainTeed Covers Landmark

CertainTeed backs Landmark with a lifetime limited transferable warranty, and CertainTeed includes 10-year SureStart protection and a 25-year StreakFighter algae-resistance warranty. CertainTeed also provides a 15-year, 110 mph wind-resistance warranty on Landmark, with an upgrade to 160 mph available when the roof is installed with specified CertainTeed starter and hip & ridge accessories over a full tear-off.

Manufacturer warranties are provided and administered by CertainTeed, and the full terms, exclusions, and transfer rules are CertainTeed's to define — we recommend reading the current warranty document before you decide, and we are happy to walk through it with you at your estimate.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does a Class 3 impact rating actually mean?

It is the shingle's score under UL 2218, a standardized test that drops steel balls of increasing size onto the shingle to simulate hail. Ratings run from Class 1 to Class 4, with Class 4 the highest. Class 3 is a solid rating for typical Central Ohio weather; if your neighborhood takes regular hail, ask us to also price a Class 4 line such as Landmark Premium or Landmark ClimateFlex.

How is Landmark different from Landmark PRO?

Both are dual-layer architectural shingles with the same UL Class A fire rating, UL 2218 Class 3 impact rating, and 110 mph wind resistance. Landmark PRO is a heavier laminate, comes in CertainTeed's Max Def color palette with richer blends, and carries a 30-year StreakFighter algae-resistance warranty from CertainTeed instead of 25 years. We price both on your estimate so you can see the difference in real dollars.

Who handles the manufacturer coverage on my shingles?

Manufacturer warranties are provided and administered by CertainTeed — ask us which manufacturer's warranty applies to the shingles on your estimate, and review the full terms on CertainTeed's warranty page.
