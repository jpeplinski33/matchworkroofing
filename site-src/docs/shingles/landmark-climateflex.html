The Look

What Landmark ClimateFlex Looks Like — and Who It Suits

On the roof, ClimateFlex keeps the familiar dimensional Landmark profile — staggered tabs and shadow lines that suit everything from Dublin colonials to newer New Albany builds. The difference is in the asphalt itself: it is blended with rubberizing polymers, so the shingle bends under impact and cold rather than turning brittle.

It suits two kinds of Central Ohio homeowners especially. The first is anyone in a hail corridor — spring storms roll through the Columbus metro most years, and the UL 2218 Class 4 rating is the highest impact classification the industry test awards. The second is anyone roofing late in the season: the shingle's cold-weather pliability gives it an advantage when a tear-off lands in November instead of June.

Some insurers offer premium discounts for Class 4 roofs — ask your agent what your policy offers, and ask us to price ClimateFlex against Landmark Premium and NorthGate ClimateFlex so you can compare the Class 4 options side by side.

Manufacturer Warranty

How CertainTeed Covers Landmark ClimateFlex

CertainTeed backs Landmark ClimateFlex with a lifetime limited transferable warranty on residential applications, and CertainTeed includes 10-year SureStart protection and a 25-year StreakFighter algae-resistance warranty. CertainTeed also provides a 15-year, 110 mph wind-resistance warranty on Landmark ClimateFlex, with an upgrade to 160 mph available when the roof is installed with specified CertainTeed starter and hip & ridge accessories over a full tear-off.

Manufacturer warranties are provided and administered by CertainTeed, and the full terms, exclusions, and transfer rules are CertainTeed's to define — we recommend reading the current warranty document before you decide, and we are happy to walk through it with you at your estimate.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does "rubberized, Class 4" actually mean?

The asphalt in ClimateFlex is modified with rubberizing polymers, so the shingle flexes under impact instead of cracking. Class 4 is its score under UL 2218, a standardized test that drops steel balls of increasing size onto the shingle to simulate hail — Class 4 is the highest of the four classes. For hail-prone Central Ohio neighborhoods, it is the strongest published impact rating a shingle can carry, and some insurers offer premium discounts for Class 4 roofs.

How is Landmark ClimateFlex different from NorthGate ClimateFlex?

Both are rubberized polymer-modified shingles with a UL 2218 Class 4 impact rating. NorthGate ClimateFlex is CertainTeed's premium rubberized line, offered in the richer Max Def color palette, while Landmark ClimateFlex brings the same rubberized approach to the classic Landmark profile. We can price both on the same estimate so you can compare directly.

Who handles the manufacturer coverage on my shingles?

Manufacturer warranties are provided and administered by CertainTeed — ask us which manufacturer's warranty applies to the shingles on your estimate, and review the full terms on CertainTeed's warranty page.
