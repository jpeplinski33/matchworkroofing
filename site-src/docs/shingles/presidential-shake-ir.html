What It Looks Like

A Wood-Shake Roof That Shrugs Off Hail Season

From the curb, Presidential Shake IR is indistinguishable from the standard Presidential look — staggered, sculpted tabs with rustic edges that read as hand-split cedar rather than a flat asphalt pattern. The difference is underneath: CertainTeed adds a fiberglass backing engineered to absorb impact without cracking.

That construction earns the top UL 2218 Class 4 impact rating, which matters in Central Ohio, where spring and summer storm cells put hail on roofs from Hilliard to Pickerington most years.

Who It Suits

Storm-Weary Homes With Big, Visible Roofs

Presidential Shake IR makes the most sense on homes where the roof is a major design feature — the traditional two-stories of Upper Arlington, custom builds in Dublin and New Albany — and where the owner has already been through a hail claim or two and wants the toughest version of the look they love.

Some insurers offer premium discounts for Class 4 impact-rated roofs. Ask your insurance agent what your policy offers, and ask us to price Presidential Shake IR alongside standard Presidential Shake so you can weigh the difference.

Manufacturer Coverage

The CertainTeed Warranty on Presidential Shake IR

CertainTeed backs Presidential Shake IR with a lifetime limited transferable warranty against manufacturing defects on residential applications, and a 50-year limited transferable warranty on group-owned or commercial applications. CertainTeed also includes 10-year SureStart protection, a 30-year StreakFighter algae-resistance warranty, and a 15-year 110 mph wind-resistance warranty, with an upgrade to 130 mph available when the roof is installed with CertainTeed starter and CertainTeed hip & ridge accessories.

Manufacturer warranties are provided and administered by CertainTeed. Read the full terms on CertainTeed's warranty page or in CertainTeed's 2026 asphalt shingle limited warranty (PDF), and ask us which manufacturer warranty applies to the shingles on your estimate.

Matchwork's own workmanship coverage is described separately on your proposal.

Frequently Asked Questions

What makes Presidential Shake IR different from standard Presidential Shake?

The impact build. Standard Presidential Shake carries a UL 2218 Class 3 impact rating, while Presidential Shake IR adds a fiberglass backing engineered for impact absorption and carries a Class 4 rating — the highest class. The sculpted wood-shake look and the CertainTeed warranty stack are otherwise shared across the line.

Will a Class 4 roof lower my insurance premium in Ohio?

Sometimes. A number of insurers offer discounts for UL 2218 Class 4 impact-rated roofs, but the amount and eligibility rules vary by carrier and policy. We do not set insurance pricing — call your agent before your estimate and ask what a Class 4 roof is worth on your policy, then we will price the options side by side.

Who handles the manufacturer coverage on my shingles?

Manufacturer warranties are provided and administered by CertainTeed — ask us which manufacturer's warranty applies to the shingles on your estimate, and review the full terms on CertainTeed's warranty page.
