The trim at your roof edge is the first place water reports a problem, and the last place anyone looks.

Fascia rot repair

Fascia rot repair, start to finish

How rot starts behind a gutter

Almost every rotted fascia we replace in Columbus was killed by its own gutter. Water gets behind the back edge where there is no drip edge, or where the drip edge stops short of the channel, and runs down the board instead of into it. A blocked gutter holds that water against the wood for days, and spikes driven through the gutter leave holes that wick water into the end grain.

How we find it

Paint lifting, dark staining, a soft spot at a fastener hole, a gutter pulling away from the roofline, a board that sounds wrong when tapped. We drop the gutter section and probe: sound, softened or gone, and the repair depends on which.

Sistering versus full replacement

Localised rot with solid rafter tails: we cut out the soft section and sister in new wood, or wrap the board in aluminium so the wood stops being the wearing surface. Rot along the whole elevation, or soft tails: the board comes off, we cut back to sound framing and scab in new tails first. A new board over a rotted tail is a straight line over a sagging roof edge.

Aluminium versus wood

Aluminium coil wrap suits most Columbus houses: no rot, no paint, colour-matched to gutter and trim. It dents, and it hides what is behind it, so we never wrap wood we have not inspected. Wood is the call where the profile is deep or an HOA expects painted trim; cellular PVC works at wet locations.

What we warrant

Matchwork warrants the repairs we performed, on the terms in your proposal. A repair is not a warranty on the rest of the roof, the siding, the gutters or the masonry we did not touch, and we will never present it as one.

The cause

How gutter placement causes trim rot

Fix the board without fixing the gutter and you are scheduling the same repair for a few years out.

  • Gutter set below the roof plane. If the back edge sits under the drip edge instead of behind it, every drop runs down the fascia. A placement error, and common on re-hung gutters.
  • Wrong slope. A gutter that does not fall toward the outlet holds water and debris against the wood through every freeze-thaw cycle.
  • Downspout dumping at the wall. A downspout ending at the foundation splashes back onto trim and siding. Extensions and underground tie-ins are the fix — downspout extensions and drainage.

A fascia repair that ignores the gutter above it is temporary. When the gutter is spent we say so and put both scopes in the proposal. Gutters colour-matched to roof, trim and brick covers replacement.

Soffit

Soffit repair, replacement and ventilation

Soffit fails for a short list of reasons: an exhaust duct emptying into the soffit cavity, ice dam water backing under the shingles, attic condensation, wasps, and ladders. Painted wood rots, vinyl cracks and sags, aluminium lasts but dents.

Vented soffit is the attic's intake, so we do not seal it off to make a problem disappear. If ridge vent and intake are not balanced the deck suffers, and that conversation belongs with the roof. When soffit rot goes with a worn roof edge, roof replacement in Columbus and Dublin fixes both.

Trim

Window, corner and water-table trim

Trim is where a siding job reads as careful or rushed. We re-detail casings, corner posts, frieze board and water table in the material the wall wants: fiber cement trim on cement board, engineered wood or cellular PVC for a deep profile, aluminium coil where a wrap is smarter.

  • Head flashing above every opening, so water runs onto the trim face and never behind it.
  • Kickout flashing where a roof slope ends against a wall — the most skipped detail in Ohio.
  • Casings proud of the siding face, joints and end grain sealed before the board goes up.
  • Trim colour chosen at the house against brick and body colour, per the Matchwork method.

Wider trim work belongs on siding repair in Columbus; the material picture is on the siding hub.

Common questions

What homeowners ask about fascia and soffit

Is a soft fascia board urgent?

Soft enough to act on this season. The board holds your gutter, and a gutter pulling away takes the fascia with it.

Can you wrap my fascia instead of replacing it?

Yes, when the wood behind it is sound. We inspect, repair what is soft, then wrap in colour-matched aluminium. We never wrap over rot.

My inspection report named fascia rot. What now?

Send it over. We read the finding, look at the board, and write a scope that answers what the inspector saw. The realtor program covers the documentation.
