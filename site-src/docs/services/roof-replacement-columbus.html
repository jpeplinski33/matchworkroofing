The MATCHWORK™ Standard

We Install Complete Roofing Systems, Not Just Shingles

The shingle is the part you see. A roof that lasts is everything under and around it too. We install the CertainTeed Integrity Roof System (CertainTeed's matched underlayments, starter, shingles, ventilation, and hip and ridge caps) plus the deck work, edge metal, and flashing every roof needs. Your written proposal lists every part.

01. Decking

Inspected After Tear-Off

With the old roof torn off, the whole deck is checked. Soft or rotted boards and delaminated plywood are replaced before anything covers them.

02. Ice & Water Barrier

Self-Adhering Membrane

Waterproofing underlayment at the eaves, in the valleys, and around chimneys, skylights, and pipes, where ice dams and wind-driven rain cause leaks. At the eaves, Ohio's residential code runs it from the lowest edge of the roof to at least 24 inches inside the outside face of the wall, measured up the slope, and at least 36 inches up from the eave on roofs 8/12 or steeper.

03. Underlayment

Synthetic, Water-Resistant

Covers the rest of the deck as a second barrier against water beneath the shingles.

04. Drip Edge

Metal at Eaves and Rakes

Protects the edge of the deck and directs water away from the fascia and into the gutter.

05. Starter Strip

Sealed First Course

A starter course along the eaves and rakes so the first row of shingles is sealed at the edge, where wind tries to lift it.

06. Shingles

The CertainTeed Line You Choose

Landmark PRO on most homes, or a designer slate-look or shake-look line, chosen with you to suit the house.

07. Flashing

Walls, Chimneys, and Pipes

Step flashing where the roof meets a wall, flashing and counter-flashing at the chimney, and boots at plumbing vents. These joints are where many roof leaks start.

08. Hip & Ridge Caps

Finished Peaks

Purpose-made cap shingles close off the hips and ridges in colors made to go with the shingles on the roof.

09. Ventilation

Balanced Intake & Exhaust

Intake at the eaves or soffits and exhaust at or near the ridge, planned as a pair so air moves through the attic, keeping it cooler in summer and drier in winter.

Protecting your landscaping, siding, and driveway during tear-off, and cleaning up afterward, are part of every MATCHWORK™ roof.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you provide roof replacement estimates in Columbus, OH?

We measure the roof, check the flashing, vents, chimneys and decking, and write a line-item proposal with the shingle line and colors you choose.

How long does a roof replacement take?

It depends on roof size and pitch, how many layers come off, decking repairs and weather. We go over the expected schedule with you when we review your proposal.

What affects the scope and cost of a roof replacement?

Roof size and pitch, the number of existing shingle layers, decking repairs, flashing and chimney work, ventilation changes, and the shingle line you choose. Your written proposal itemizes each of these.

What are the steps in the roof replacement process?

It starts with a roof assessment that reviews the roof dimensions, pitch, and details that affect the scope. Next you choose materials and colors, with physical samples reviewed against your exterior stone, brick, siding, and trim. You then receive a written proposal listing materials, components, and price, and the work follows the agreed scope.

What does the written proposal include?

The written proposal lists the materials, components and price before you commit, including the underlayment, flashing, edge metal and ventilation that go on with your shingles. Protecting your landscaping, siding and driveway, and cleaning up afterward, are part of the job.

How do tear-off and decking affect a roof replacement?

The number of existing shingle layers and any decking repairs affect the scope and cost, and your written proposal itemizes both. Tear-off and decking condition also affect timing.

Know What to Expect.

Your written proposal lays out the full scope and price before you commit.
