Technical Standards

The 4 Forensic Mismatch Factors

Insurance companies often claim 'close enough' shingles can be used for repairs. We document the precise physical and optical differences that fail Ohio's reasonable uniformity standard:

📏

Dimensional Mismatch

English vs. Metric sizing. An older 36" x 12" shingle cannot interlock with a modern 39-3/8" x 13-1/4" metric shingle without tearing surrounding shingles and invalidating wind warranties.

🎨

Granule Color Fade

Years of UV exposure oxidize surface granules. Placing a brand-new factory-fresh shingle creates a stark checkerboard defect that destroys curb appeal and home resale value.

🚫

Product Discontinuation

Manufacturers regularly retire older shingle molds (e.g. CertainTeed Horizon, Atlas Chalet, GAF Timberline 30). Once discontinued, replacement stock is legally unavailable.

🔨

Brittleness / Repairability

Bending aged shingles to insert new nails causes micro-fracturing along the asphalt mat (failing the standard ASTM D3462 pliability test), causing further structural damage during repair attempts.
