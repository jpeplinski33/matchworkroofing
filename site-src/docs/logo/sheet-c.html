Direction C — wordmark-led, craft feel

Three concepts. Marks at 16 / 32 / 64 / 200 px; lockups on light and on navy; each dropped into a mock header (light and dark) and the footer bar. Palette navy #1a2a5e · amber #f5a524 / #f8c65c · white. All directions

C1 — Pitch W

MATCHWORK set in Sora 700 with the W rebuilt as a mitred double pitch; the gable-shaped gap beneath the centre vertex fills amber, so the only ornament is a roof reading inside the name itself. The W alone becomes the favicon, and at 16 px it still reads as a W rather than a house icon — the amber notch survives as a warm dot.

C2 — Ridge tick

Sentence-case 'Matchwork' in Sora 700 — friendlier, closer to how the crew says it — with one amber ridge-line chevron sitting over the ascender of the t like a cap over a rafter. The mark isolates the t as one geometric glyph under the same chevron, so the favicon shares the exact gesture of the wordmark without any drawn illustration.

C3 — Stacked plate

A two-line plate: MATCHWORK letterspaced in Sora 700 over a hairline rule with a short amber centre bar, then ROOFING tracked wide in Inter 600 — the most 'brass nameplate on a good house' of the three. The mark keeps the plate's logic — a pitched M over the amber bar — and is the most conventional, which is also its risk.
