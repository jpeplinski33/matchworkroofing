Matchwork Roofing · logo concepts · set A

Monogram-led marks

Three directions, each drawn as paths only in a 64×64 box, in the site palette (navy #1a2a5e, amber #f5a524 / #f8c65c, white). Every concept ships a light-ground mark, a navy-ground inverse, and both lockups. The wordmark keeps the live site's MATCHWORK. / ARCHITECTURAL ROOFING & RESTORATION construction, which is the part of the current header that already reads as confident.

A1

Pitchline M

The M is built from roof planes, not letter strokes: near-vertical outer stems for the gable ends, two 52-degree rafters meeting in a valley, and an amber eave bar that gives the whole thing a floor. It carries no container, so it sits straight on the paper ground beside the wordmark the owner already likes, and the bar is the one element heavy enough to survive a favicon.

MR keystone

This keeps the live site's navy MR chip -- the piece of the current identity the owner reads as confident -- and adds a single amber ridge line over the letters so the block says roofing instead of initials. The plate brings its own contrast, so it holds on photography, on navy, and as an app icon or map pin without any special handling.

A3

Coursework M

Two identical shingle tabs, offset half a tab across and dropped one course down -- exactly how architectural shingles are laid -- lap over each other, and the notch between their feet plus the deep valley where they cross is what makes the M. The navy-over-amber lap states the match test in the mark itself, but the lap is also the whole idea, so this is the concept that needs size to work.
