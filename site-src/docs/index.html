05 Storm Damage

Storm or Hail Damage? The File Starts Here.

After wind or hail, MATCHWORK™ inspects and photographs every item of damage, writes the full scope as a line-by-line estimate, meets your adjuster on the roof, and shows where the carrier's estimate falls short. A sample of your shingle can go to an independent lab such as ITEL to identify the product and confirm whether it is still made.

Storm Damage Roofing → How Shingle Matching Works → The Code Shelf →

Ohio Wrote a Rule About Matching. We Named the Company After It.

Ohio Adm. Code 3901-1-54(I)(1)(b)

“When an interior or exterior loss requires replacement of an item and the replaced item does not match the quality, color or size of the item suffering the loss, the insurer shall replace as much of the item as to result in a reasonably comparable appearance.”

Weathered shingles and discontinued products mean a spot repair often will not match. When it will not, we document the mismatch and put this rule in front of your carrier. That documentation is our work, and we do not hand it to you to carry alone.

For policies that pay on a replacement cost basis.

Read the rule at codes.ohio.gov
