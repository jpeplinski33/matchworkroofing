The Matchwork Standard

Taking Care of Your Home

A well-planned roof considers the home as a whole: the materials, the details at walls and chimneys, and the way water leaves the building.

Materials That Fit Your Home

Help choosing shingle colors and styles that work with your brick, stone, siding, and trim.

Complete Roof System

Underlayment, flashing, roof edges, and ventilation all matter. The scope should address how these components work with the selected shingles.

Property Protection

Property protection and cleanup belong in the project scope, alongside the materials and installation details.

Four Trades, One Contractor

Roofing, siding, gutters, and masonry handled by one company, so the places where they meet, like flashing, drip edge, and chimney counter-flashing, are detailed together.

Storm Damage

Storm or Hail Damage? Start With Careful Documentation.

After wind or hail, the repair scope should reflect the whole roof system, not just the shingles that came off. Matchwork inspects, photographs what we find, and explains your repair or replacement options in plain terms. When a shingle has been discontinued, a sample can be sent to an independent lab such as ITEL to identify the product.

Storm Damage Roofing → How ITEL Shingle Matching Works →

Why Matching Matters

Weathered shingles and discontinued products mean a spot repair often will not match in color or size. Documenting the product and its condition helps define a repair scope that looks right.
