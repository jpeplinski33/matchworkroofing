Four trades, one crew

The whole envelope, from one company

A Columbus house does not fail in one trade. Water gets in at the chimney, runs behind the siding, rots the fascia and dumps out of a gutter against the footing. We work on all four.

Roofing

Architectural, designer and Class 4 shingles, standing seam and copper accents, slate, cedar shake, and flat or low-slope work. Tear-off, deck inspection, ice-and-water at eaves and valleys, new flashing rather than reused.

Siding

James Hardie fiber cement and LP SmartSide first, vinyl where vinyl is the honest answer, board and batten, cedar shake panel, and the soffit, fascia and trim rot behind a failing wall.

Gutters

Five-inch and six-inch seamless aluminium, half-round copper where the house calls for it, colour chosen against roof, trim and brick, plus downspout extensions and tie-ins that move water off the footing.

Masonry

Chimney repair, crowns, caps and rebuilds, tuckpointing with mortar matched to the age of the wall, brick matching, stone veneer, stucco and the roof-to-wall flashing that saves it. Envelope work, not hardscape.

The Matchwork standard

Everything we install has to match something

A roof is not a colour on a website. It is a colour on your house, in your light, next to your brick.

Sample boards come out of the truck and go up against your siding, brick, stone and trim in your own daylight. We look at the wall in the morning and again with the sun behind the house, because the same shingle reads brown at nine and grey at four. Only then does anything get ordered.

That habit runs across all four trades: shingle to brick, siding to trim, gutter to the whole palette, mortar to an aged wall. The reasoning, and the honest limits when a line has been discontinued or a blend reformulated, are in the Matchwork method.

How we work

From first call to a clean site

A short, unhurried sequence. No pressure, no on-the-spot signature.

  1. We measure the whole house

    Roof planes and pitch; wall elevations and what is behind them; gutter runs and where the water goes; chimney, crown and mortar joints. We photograph what we find, including the parts that are fine.

  2. We match in person, at your house

    Sample boards against your brick, siding, stone and trim in your own daylight, at more than one hour of the day. Nobody picks an exterior colour from a phone screen.

  3. You get a written proposal

    One document, in plain English: measurements, exact product line and colour, underlayment and ice-and-water coverage, ventilation, flashing, gutter and downspout details, masonry scope, what we are not doing. Manufacturer warranty options are explained at your consultation.

  4. Our own crew does the work

    The same people across the trades, so the roofer and the gutter installer are not arguing about whose flashing detail it is. Landscaping covered, rotten sheathing shown to you before it is covered up.

  5. We leave the site clean

    Ground protection, debris tarped rather than dropped, a magnet run over the drive and lawn at the end of every work day, and a final walk with photographs and paperwork in one place.
