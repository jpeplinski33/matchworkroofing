Our Restoration Superpower

Severe Storm or Hail Damage? We Hold Insurance Carriers to Ohio Matching Standards.

Most retail contractors don't understand insurance law, while storm chasers don't care about craftsmanship. Matchwork Roofing bridges the gap: we bring master architectural craftsmanship to every job, and when storms hit, we utilize **Ohio Administrative Code 3901-1-54** and laboratory ITEL testing to ensure insurance companies pay for 100% full roof replacements rather than mismatched spot patches.
