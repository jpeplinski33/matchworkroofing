Frequently Asked Questions

Do you help with insurance claims?

We can provide inspection photos and shingle identification results so the repair scope can be discussed on the facts. Decisions about coverage rest with your insurer and your policy.

Do you replace gutters and siding?

Matchwork Roofing & Exteriors replaces and repairs siding as part of a roof project or on its own. Matchwork Roofing & Exteriors replaces and repairs gutter systems, installs gutter guards that keep leaves and debris out, and can include both in a roofing estimate.
