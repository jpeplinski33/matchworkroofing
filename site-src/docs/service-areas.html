Primary Service Areas

Each of these communities has its own architectural character, its own HOA expectations, and its own storm history. Explore the hub closest to your home.

Columbus, OH

From German Village brick to post-war ranches and new-build infill, Columbus roofs run every profile and age. We measure, color-match, and replace to the architecture that's already there.

Dublin, OH

Muirfield Village, Tartan Fields, and Ballantrae carry strict architectural review standards. We spec shingle color and profile to pass ARB the first time — and to match the homes beside you.

New Albany, OH

Georgian brick, white fence lines, and steep multi-facet estate roofs. Luxury slate-profile shingles and hand-fabricated copper detailing built to hold the neighborhood's uniform look.

New Albany Roofing →

Powell, OH

Wooded Olentangy-corridor lots mean shaded north slopes, algae streaking, and heavy tree debris in valleys. We build the full system — leak barriers, breathable underlayment, balanced ridge venting.

Powell Roofing →

Upper Arlington, OH

Tudor, Colonial, and mid-century homes with slate and tile heritage. Older discontinued shingle lines make matching hard after a storm — which is exactly where ITEL testing earns its keep.
